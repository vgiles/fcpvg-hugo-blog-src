About

vincent giles writing

vincent giles ~1 min read

I’m a composer and improviser, ttrpg game master, and general rastabout living just out of Naarm (Melbourne). My creative energies are focused on game-making, storytelling, abstract music, and reconnecting with the things I loved about creativity as a child. In my life to date, I’ve done many things: taught a lot of music to a lot of people over more than 20 years, completed a PhD, done live sound, done studio sound, composed and published music internationally, worked in retail, worked as a music journalist, published academic papers, published non-academic writing, co-founded and co-ran an experimental music festival that went from half a day and local to over a week and international, have had my music performed around the world, worked in higher education, forgotten to have hobbies, found hobbies, travelled to multiple parts of the world, and, functionally, figured it all out along the way. As such, despite a bunch of formal and informal education, I consider myself largely autodidactic in music due to how my brain works. Thankfully, the formal education was friendly towards that at the time. All of this is to give context to what I’m doing now. It’s taken me a long time to realise just how much all of this life stuff contributes to the creative stuff, and I’m excited to share a bit of it as I go. Mostly. I’m also nervous about that.

That’s what this website is about. It’s connected to the others as well, but this one is about sharing things.
